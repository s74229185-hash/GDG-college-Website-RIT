Why CampusSync

Event posters get lost in group chats and students miss what they care about. CampusSync fixes that.

One shared calendar

Every department, club and GDG event sits in a single list that anyone can search and filter.

Seats in two taps

Register with a name and email, see seats left, and cancel when plans change.

Spread the word

Add any event to Google Calendar or share it on WhatsApp so friends join too.

About CampusSync

CampusSync is a student-built project for the GDG on Campus Website Building Challenge. It brings Rajeev Institute of Technology, the Computer Science and Engineering department and GDG on Campus RIT Hassan into one shared calendar so no event goes unnoticed.
